About Mr. Informer

Mr. Informer tracks tech, AI, cybersecurity, and hardware news from a wide set of public RSS sources so readers don't have to check them all separately.

How briefings are made

Most posts on this site are short, AI-assisted summaries of a single third-party news story, published with an outbound link to the original report and the source it came from. This is disclosed openly rather than presented as original investigative reporting — the goal is to help you find and understand real coverage faster, not to replace the outlets doing that reporting.

What we don't do

We don't publish invented headlines, fabricated statistics, or fake engagement numbers. If there's no genuine new story to summarize in a given cycle, we simply don't publish one.

Corrections and tips

Spotted something wrong, or have a story tip? Use the contact page or the whistleblower dropbox linked from the homepage.
