Regulatory scrutiny over online child safety is intensifying as governments examine whether popular digital platforms are doing enough to protect young users from bad actors. When safety regulators like Australia's eSafety investigate major interactive ecosystems like Roblox for compliance failures, it signals a growing push for platform accountability regarding adult-child interactions. Technical context

The underlying issue involves platform compliance with legislation such as Australia's Online Safety Act, specifically concerning the implementation of sufficient technical and systemic measures to prevent contact between adults and children under 16.
