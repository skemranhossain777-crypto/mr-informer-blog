Technical context

The Saily Ultra product is an embedded subscriber identity module, or eSIM, which allows users to download and activate cellular plans digitally without swapping a physical SIM card. This technology facilitates continuous network access as users move across different international borders. According to the source report, the service provides uninterrupted connectivity alongside additional perks, though it comes at a high price point.
