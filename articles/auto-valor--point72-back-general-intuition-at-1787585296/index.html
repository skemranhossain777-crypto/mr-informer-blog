The massive valuation and high-profile backing of General Intuition highlight the intense investor appetite for companies combining artificial intelligence with physical automation. As AI startups increasingly push beyond digital interfaces into robotics and spatial reasoning, foundational technologies that help machines navigate the physical world are becoming a central battleground in the tech industry. Technical context

General Intuition is developing a foundation model designed to train generalized AI agents on how to move through space and time. Foundation models serve as core architectures that can be adapted to a wide variety of downstream tasks, which in this case involves translating digital intelligence into physical movement. By securing a $6 billion pre-money valuation in talks with investors like Valor Ventures, Point72 Ventures, and Seven Seven Six, the startup is scaling its capabilities as it expands its focus into robotics.
